### Rights notice and removal requests

If material used on this site concerns your rights, or if its source or attribution is incorrect, please email [hello+travlog@wei-lee.me](mailto:hello+travlog@wei-lee.me?subject=Copyright%20and%20Rights%20Notice). Please include, where possible:

* The URL of the article and the location of the material
* A description or screenshot identifying the material
* Your relationship to the rights in the material
* Your requested action, such as adding a source, correcting attribution, or removing the material

I will review the notice and respond as soon as reasonably possible. If there is a credible concern, I will remove or adjust the material while reviewing it.

This page describes this site's content policy and contact process. It does not constitute legal advice.
